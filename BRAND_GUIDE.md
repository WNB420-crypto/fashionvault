# FashionVault — Connected by design

## Identity
A connected fashion lab: floating clothing stations above an olive circuit board. The platform should feel like an invitation to create, with clear, practical controls beneath the visual energy.

## Palette
| Token | Color | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Carbon | #10120D | Main backgrounds, dark logo details |
| Olive | #657238 | Surfaces, outlines, supporting accents |
| Signal | #F5D328 | Primary actions, selected stations, circuit paths |
| Mist | #B6BE92 | Secondary highlights |
| Ivory | #F3F0DF | Primary text |

Use dark Carbon text on Signal buttons, and Ivory text on Carbon surfaces. Avoid yellow body copy or olive text over similar olive backgrounds. Cyan, violet and magenta belong to the previous identity and are retired.

## Typography
Space Grotesk: headlines, figures, brand labels. Inter: body text, navigation and controls. Use medium-weight display headings with comfortable negative space. Fonts load through Google Fonts with system fallbacks.

## Logo
assets/fashionvault-logo.png is the selected circuit-board logo. Preserve the proportions. The full detailed logo suits larger placements; use the adjacent typed wordmark for legibility in compact navigation. Leave clear space around the mark. Do not stretch, recolor or add new glow. The visual board includes concept variations and mockups; those are not separate production vector assets.

All supplied graphics are raster PNGs. Campaign and product art are illustrative, not actual catalog products or client inventory.

## Motion
Slow hero drift, subtle active-station floating and signal pulse. Hover details reveal depth. The Motion toggle pauses animation; reduced-motion OS preferences override animation. Avoid rapid flashing, distracting loops and blocking transitions.

## Voice
Confident, creative and direct. Examples: “Your vision. Fully connected.”, “Build your next drop”, “Enter the vault”. Do not imply that outreach or placements happen automatically.
